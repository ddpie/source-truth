# Code of Conduct

source-truth welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

In issues, pull requests, and other project spaces:

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Give others room to ask questions and correct mistakes.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or share another person's private information.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or personal attacks.

Project maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation to keep these spaces constructive.

For a sensitive conduct report, request a private contact channel from a maintainer without posting the
incident details or identifying information publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
